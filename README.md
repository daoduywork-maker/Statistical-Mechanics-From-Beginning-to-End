## Lecture Notes: *Statistical Mechanics Rediscovered Through the Lens of the Entropy Maximization Principle*

### Description

Statistical mechanics, physical chemistry, and their quantum extensions underpin much of materials science research. Textbooks on these subjects often build on physical heuristics and verbal arguments, which leaves the underlying mathematical structure implicit.

These notes take the opposite route. They start from a single mathematical principle, the maximization of the Gibbs-Shannon entropy under constraints (MaxEnt), and derive the formalism from it. Each ensemble follows from a different choice of constraints, and each thermodynamic quantity appears as a multiplier or a potential with a precise mathematical role. Once the formulation is in place, the notes generalize it to physically relevant observables and phenomena.
